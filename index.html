DELHI PROTESTS: 2,500+ DETAINED

A supplied report says more than 2,500 protesters were detained during demonstrations demanding the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar over alleged electoral irregularities.

October 11, 2026 · Claims attributed to supplied report

Supreme Court hearing

The supplied report says the Supreme Court scheduled a special hearing on petitions challenging the detentions and reported restrictions on public transport. The supplied text does not include the final outcome of that hearing.

Key developments

According to the report, CJP leaders Abhijeet Dipke, Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka were released on Sunday morning, as stated by the party’s legal head. The report also says Dhruv Rathee was released from detention in Gurugram.

UN and government response

The supplied report says the UN human rights office expressed concern over reports of mass detentions, while the Indian government rejected the body’s criticism.

Why it matters

The events raise questions about peaceful assembly, preventive detention and democratic rights. Readers should distinguish allegations, attributed statements and independently verified findings.
